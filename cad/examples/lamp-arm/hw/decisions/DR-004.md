# DR-004: Bushings, lampholder insert and retaining rings for production

- Date: 2026-10-27
- Status: decided by the user on 2026-10-27. Items without a basis stay open; see Open items.

## Context

The user decided on 2026-10-27 that the PETG pivot bores in LMP-0001 and LMP-0004 take
heat-set bushings with toleranced bores instead of printed fits. The pins LMP-0005 to
LMP-0007 are made to drawing in stainless A2 with the supplier open. The DIN 471 rings
become catalogue parts, and the head's Ø9 thread-forming pilot becomes a heat-set M10x1
brass insert. The aluminium links are now supplied as machined and deburred. Only mechanical
parts are in scope; the lampholder and wiring are not.

DR-003 left both PETG pivots as 5.2 mm printed bores, a "5 H11 plus 0.2 mm allowance" that
no FDM process holds. A search on 2026-10-27 found no catalogue plain-bore insert that is
documented for heat-set installation, has a bore near 5 mm, and publishes its bore limits.
McMaster-Carr's heat-set, heat-staking and compression-limiter pages had none. The SPIROL
heat/ultrasonic range, PEM SI, Tappex, E-Z LOK and Böllhoff/Bossard BN 1052 and BN 1934 are
all threaded. The search also found no catalogue M10x1 heat-set insert with published
installation data. In-saiL lists its M10 x 12.7 insert as M10x1.5. CNC Kitchen, ruthex, PSM
Sonic-Lok and Tappex publish M10 sizes without a pitch.

The user then reviewed the proposals below and decided, on 2026-10-27:

1. Accept every tolerance proposal, and add ISO 13715 edge values and surface finishes.
2. A standard part ordered by its standard designation (DIN 471 - 5x0.6) needs no manufacturer
   part number.
3. Keep the scale policy.
4. Never commit the vendor PDFs to this repository; keep the url, revision and sha256 references.

## Decision

**Bushings.** Both PETG pivots take SPIROL knurled brass compression limiters, M4 size, as
plain bushings. CMPL 4 X 8 EK CL8000 (LMP-0008) goes in the base boss. The headed
CMPL 4 X 6 EK CL8100 (LMP-0009) goes in the head knuckle, its head in a +Y counterbore as a
brass thrust face toward the upper link. SPIROL publishes a 5.05/5.15 bore, a 7.56/7.72
body, a 7.97 nominal knurl, a 9.35/9.60 x 1.00 head, L +0/-0.15, a 7.73/7.81 recommended
hole and 360 brass with a plain finish. The pin journals stay 5 f7 and run in that bore with
0.060/0.172 mm diametral clearance (STK-101). That is looser than the 5 H8/f7 elbow, but the
vendor controls it; the old printed bore gave about 0.22 mm and no control. SPIROL documents
press-in and mould-in installation, not heat-setting, so heat installation is our process
choice. Its datasheet says the knurl is always larger than the largest hole, so the hole
limits hold either way.

The alternative was an ISO 2795 sintered-bronze sleeve 5 x 8 x 8, such as Bolton AM-050808.
Pressed into a rigid H7 housing it gives an H7 bore for an f7 shaft. Bolton documents no
polymer or heat-set installation, the plain OD gives no torque retention in PETG, and a
press fit into a printed hole is the printed fit the user rejected. It stays the fallback if
first articles show the CL8000 clearance is too loose.

**Seats.** Each seat is Ø7.73/7.81, drilled or reamed after printing, because an FDM
bureau's ±0.2 mm cannot hold a 0.08 mm band. The base seat runs z 21.5 to 29.5 with the
bushing flush at both ends. The head seat runs through the 7 mm knuckle with a Ø10.0 x 1.0
counterbore for the CL8100 head.

**Lampholder insert.** LMP-0010 uses the In-saiL QT-M10x12.7-OD12.6 envelope with an M10x1
thread to order. In-saiL's drawing LF646 rev A (2013-05-04) gives OD 12.60 ±0.12, pilot
11.80 ±0.05 and L 12.70 ±0.12, and its product page gives a 11.9/12.0 hole and a 6.0 minimum
wall, for heat-staking or ultrasonic installation. The record first read OD and L as published
without tolerances; the vendor sheet now carries LF646's limits. The socket boss grows from
r 7 to r 12.5 and its face drops from z 54 to z 50. The blind hole is Ø11.95 ±0.05 and 13.7
deep, the insert length plus the 1 mm CNC Kitchen recommends, which leaves a 2.3 mm roof. The
thread is M10x1-6H, the ISO 965-1 default class, because the vendor publishes no class for the
special. In-saiL must confirm the M10x1 special before release.

**Rings.** LMP-0011 is a DIN 471 - 5x0.6 ring in spring steel C60S, phosphated and oiled,
placed three times: `@ring-base` on LMP-0005, `@ring-elbow` on LMP-0006 and `@ring-head` on
LMP-0007 (`assembly.yaml` occurrences, BOM qty 3). Beneri gives that as the standard material
and finish for d1 3 to 27 mm. Its 1.4122 stainless ring is not standard at 5 mm. DIN 471 gives
d3 4.7 +0.04/-0.15, s 0.6 0/-0.05, a 2.5, b 1.1, d5 1.0 and d4 10.2. The groove is d2 4.8 h10
(4.752/4.800, cad-fits) with m 0.70 H13 and n min 0.3. Each ring weighs 0.081 g, 0.243 g for
the three. The free ring grips the groove root by 0.012 to 0.250 mm on diameter (STK-104). The
pins already carry this groove (DR-003). One ring is one part, so the three placements are
occurrences of LMP-0011, modelled part-local. The ring is ordered by its designation
`DIN 471 - 5x0.6` (see Standard parts); Beneri's datasheet stays its specification.

**Pins and links.** Pin geometry is unchanged. The links lose their anodize, so the 5 H7
press bores and the 5 H8 elbow bore are final as machined, with no coating on fit surfaces.

**Model and record.** Each catalogue part is modelled as an envelope. The knurl sits at its
nominal over the full length because no pilot length is published. The insert's thread is a
plain bore at the M10x1 basic minor diameter, 8.917 by ISO 724. The ring has a concentric
0.8 mm section and sits 0.005 mm off its groove and its bearing face to avoid coincident
Boolean faces. Fixed joints J-007 to J-012 carry each new part with its host; J-010 to J-012
carry the three ring occurrences. The three heat-set overlaps are declared in
`interfaces.yaml` under `limits.heat_set`.

## Edges

The edges follow ISO 13715:2017, which EN ISO 13715:2019 adopts. ISO 13715 §4.2 signs an edge
size: + permits excess material, a burr on an external edge or a passing on an internal one;
− requires removal, an edge break or an undercut. §4.3 pairs a single limit with zero. The
general indication covers every edge of undefined shape (§4.5.4), and a feature's own value is
an exception to it (§4.5.5). The sheets write the general indication as a note and an exception
in the feature's callout. ASME has no edge symbol, so in the ASME regime the same limits read as
the customary note, for example `REMOVE ALL BURRS, EDGE BREAK 0.3 MAX; INTERNAL CORNERS R0.3 MAX,
NO UNDERCUT`.

| Parts | External | Internal | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| CNC: LMP-0002, LMP-0003, LMP-0005 to LMP-0007 | −0.3: no burr, a break up to 0.3 | +0.3: a passing up to 0.3, no undercut | "As machined and deburred" (Finishes) excludes a burr. 0.3 is the lower limit of the smallest dimensioned chamfer: 0.4 ±0.1 on the pins, and 0.5 × 45° at ISO 2768-m ±0.2 (ISO 2768-1 Table 2) on the links, so no undefined edge exceeds a dimensioned one. The internal passing matches it, so a pin head's underside corner clears the 0.5 bore chamfer and the head seats on B |
| FDM: LMP-0001, LMP-0004 | −0.2 | +0.2 | The bureau's ±0.2 is the finest geometry the print holds, so a smaller edge value can be neither printed nor inspected. Burrs and stringing are removed (finish note). 0.2 is also the lower limit of the smallest dimensioned FDM chamfer, 0.4 ±0.2 on LMP-0004 |
| Pin groove, LMP-0005 to LMP-0007 (exception) | −0.02 | +0.06 | DIN 471:2011 §10.3 caps the loaded-side groove radius at 0.1 s, 0.06 for s = 0.6. The groove is only 0.1 deep (d2 4.8 on d1 5), so the general −0.3 would remove the ring's bearing flank; −0.02 keeps its mouth sharp and burr-free. 0.02 is the smallest size in the ISO 13715:2000 Annex B table, which was informative and is not in the 2017 edition |
| Bought: LMP-0008 to LMP-0011 | as supplied | as supplied | The vendor sheet states that edges are as supplied to the specification; In-saiL LF646 note 5 requires its insert free of burrs and sharp edges |

## Surface texture

Each value is an upper limit of Ra to ISO 21920-1:2021. Under ISO 21920 the default
acceptance rule is that no measured value exceeds the limit; the 16 % rule applies only where
the indication asks for it. The general indication beside the title block covers every surface without its own
symbol (§9.1.2), and the basic symbol in parentheses after it marks that some surfaces carry
their own. A feature's own symbol is indicated together with its size callout. In the ASME
regime the symbol is ASME Y14.36-2018's, with the same value in micrometres.

| Surface | Requirement | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Pin press band, LMP-0005 to LMP-0007 | Ra 0.8, material removal required | ISO 2338:1997 Table 2 gives Ra < 0.8 µm for m6 parallel pins in steel and austenitic stainless steel. The band is the same IT6 press-fit diameter, ground or precision-turned |
| Pin journal | Ra 0.8, material removal required | The same ISO 2338 value; ISO 8734:1997 Table 2 also gives Ra < 0.8 µm for hardened parallel pins. The journal runs in the SPIROL bore and the 5 H8 bore and is finished in the band's operation (Finishes) |
| Pin, general, groove included | Ra 1.6, material removal required | ISO 2338:1997 Table 2 gives Ra < 1.6 µm for h8 pins. DIN 471 and the Beneri datasheet give no groove roughness, so the groove takes the general value |
| Links LMP-0002 and LMP-0003, bores and general | OPEN | No standard clause or vendor specification in the record gives a roughness for an H7 or H8 hole or a general machined surface. ISO 2338 and ISO 8734 control only the pin, and ISO 286 and DIN 7190-1 give none. The withdrawn DIN 4766-2 lists what each process can achieve, which sets no limit |
| PETG LMP-0001 and LMP-0004, general | OPEN | No standard or vendor specification in the record gives an as-printed PETG roughness; the accessible text of ISO/ASTM 52910 and VDI 3405 Blatt 3.4 gives none. The print bureau's surface specification is needed |
| PETG bushing seats and insert hole | no own requirement | Retention comes from knurl embedment in the recommended hole (STK-102, STK-103). The SPIROL flyer 01/24 and In-saiL LF646 state no hole finish, so the seats fall under the open general texture |
| Bought: LMP-0008 to LMP-0011 | as supplied | The vendor sheet states that texture is as supplied to the specification; LF646 gives its insert Ra 1.6 |

## Standard parts

A catalogue item whose `procurement.standard` names a product standard of a recognised body (ISO,
IEC, EN, DIN, BS, JIS, ANSI, ASME, SAE, NAS or MS) is ordered by that standard's designation. Any
source that conforms to the standard can supply it, so the orderability check
(`procurement_incomplete`) asks for no manufacturer or manufacturer part number. cad-record
checks that `designation` states the standard's designation, here `DIN 471 - 5x0.6`. LMP-0011 is
such a part. LMP-0010 stays a vendor item: its M10x1 thread is a special only In-saiL can confirm.

## Consequences

The new seats leave LMP-0001 at 313 g, and the larger socket boss raises LMP-0004 from 58
to 62 g. The catalogue masses are the modelled envelopes: 2.0 g, 1.9 g and
6.7 g for LMP-0008 to LMP-0010, since the vendors publish none. The rings use Beneri's
0.081 g. Each bushing and insert needs a first-article check, because SPIROL's flyer gives
press-in and mould-in data only. Heat-set three of each into coupons. Then measure the bore,
the pull-out force and the torque retention. Removing a ring needs external circlip pliers.
The bushings and the insert cannot be replaced in the field; REQ-007 should say so.

LF646's OD limits widen the insert's diametral embedment in the 11.9/12.0 hole from the
0.60/0.70 of the nominal OD to 0.48/0.82; the coupon test covers both ends. The pins' general
Ra 1.6 also applies to the hexagon socket; a broached or eroded socket may not reach it, so the
first-article report should state the socket's measured roughness.

## Open items

  (`procurement.open`, a release blocker).
- General surface texture of LMP-0001 to LMP-0004: no value has a basis yet
  (`surface_texture_open`, a release blocker). The links need a standard or a supplier
  specification for the H7 and H8 bores and the machined surfaces; the PETG parts need the print
  bureau's surface specification.
- Every bought part, LMP-0011 included, has no supplier selected, and every part is at prototype
  revision X1; both block release, not the drawing.
- The groove's 0.06 radius comes from DIN 471:2011 §10.3; the 2026 edition was not checked.
- The insert's M10x1 thread and the heat-setting of the SPIROL sleeves are not
  vendor-documented. Revisit the insert if In-saiL declines the special. Revisit the bushings
  if first articles show bore closure or loss of retention.
