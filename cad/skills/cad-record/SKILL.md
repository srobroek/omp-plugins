# CAD Design Record

TRIGGER
+ creating or validating a hardware project's `hw/` design record
+ "what goes in the CAD design record", "validate this hardware project"
- modeling a part or assembly → `cad-model`

## Workflow

1. Create `hw/product.yaml`, `requirements.yaml`, `interfaces.yaml`, and `assembly.yaml` from the project template. Set the product's units to `mm`, choose `regime: iso|asme`, and record the current stage: `ground`, `intent`, `specify`, `concept`, `layout`, `parts`, `detail`, `bom`, `analysis`, `critique`, `reconcile`, `design_freeze`, or `release`.
2. Add `hw/parts/<PN>/part.yaml` and `model.py` for each part; use a PN matching `^[A-Z]{2,5}-[0-9]{4}$`. Put BOM rows in `hw/bom.csv`, verification evidence in `hw/verification.csv`, design rationale in `hw/decisions/DR-###.md`, and release output in `hw/releases/<rev>/manifest.json`. `hw/.cad/recheck.json` is generated by `cad-recheck` from the staged record; do not copy it from a template.
3. Add GitBuilding, DIN SPEC 3105-1, and Open Know-How files from the bundled docs templates when documenting a project.
4. Run `uv run --script skill://cad-record/scripts/validate.py <project-dir>`. Read JSON from stdout; exit 0 means no findings, 1 means record checks failed, 2 means usage or infrastructure failed.

## Rules

MUST Use revisions `X1`, `X2`, and so on for prototypes. Use released letters `A` to `Y`, excluding `I`, `O`, `Q`, `S`, `X`, and `Z`.
MUST Set `mass_target` and BOM `mass` in grams; `mass_target` may be null when unknown or not applicable.
MUST Include a `verification` method (`I` inspection, `A` analysis, `D` demonstration, `T` test) and acceptance criteria for every requirement.
MUST Define every joint's `parent` and `child` as references to occurrences included in a subassembly, and include axis, origin in mm, and minimum, nominal, and maximum travel values in its unit (degrees for rotary joints, mm for linear joints). A reference is a PN listed once in subassembly `parts`, or `"@id"` for an explicit occurrence.
MUST Give a joint whose motion follows another joint (gear or belt ratio, cam lift table, slider-crank piston or rod) a `coupling` instead of a `nominal`, with its `profiles` or `mechanisms` entry; keep its `min` and `max`. The coupling contract is cad-motion `scripts/coupling.py`; validate rejects cycles, missing drivers, and derived values outside their limits.
MUST Model one physical part once. A part placed more than once (three identical rings) is one PN with `model_frame: part_local` and one `occurrences` entry per placement, never one PN per placement; its BOM `qty` equals its occurrence count.
MUST Keep BOM columns in this order: `level,PN,rev,qty,title,material,process,make_buy,supplier,mass`, optionally followed by all of `unit,designation,manufacturer,manufacturer_part_number,supplier_part_number,specification_ref,specification_rev`. `level` starts at 0 for a numbered top assembly or 1 for an unnumbered top assembly, then increases by one per child depth; numbered assemblies with BOM children do not need a `part.yaml`. `qty` is instances per parent and `unit` is `pcs`. `mass` copies `mass_target`, and the procurement columns copy part.yaml `procurement`.

### Rigor levels

| Level | Adds |
|---|---|
| `maker` | Clear part identification, assembly interfaces, essential requirements, and basic fit/build notes. |
| `pro` (default) | Traceable requirement sources and verification criteria, controlled revisions, explicit tolerances and fits, complete EBOM, and documented analysis/review evidence. |
| `regulated` | `pro` plus controlled approvals, risk traceability, objective verification evidence, configuration and release records, and change-impact traceability required by the project's governing quality system. |

`regime: iso` applies ISO GPS conventions; `regime: asme` applies ASME dimensioning and tolerancing conventions. State the applicable regime on each product; do not mix symbol or tolerance interpretation between regimes.

## Record files

- `product.yaml`: product identity, rigor, units, drawing regime, general tolerance, and workflow state.
  - Optional `drawing`: the production drawing policy with `definition: model_and_drawing`, an explicit `projection` (`first_angle` or `third_angle`), `standards`, `default_sheet` (`sizes`, `orientation`, `scale`), named `tolerance_policies`, and `general_tolerance_policy`.
  - A tolerance policy is `kind: standard` (ISO 2768-1:1989 with a class) or `kind: limits` (`linear_mm` with its source `reference`). Each names the processes it `applies_to`.
  - `general_tolerance` must state the general policy exactly: `ISO 2768-m`, or the `linear_mm` number. `standards.dimensioning` must belong to the regime.
- `requirements.yaml`: `REQ-###` statements with rationale, source, priority, method, and verification criteria.
- `interfaces.yaml`: mating, motion, clearance, electrical, or thermal contracts. `limits.press_fit` and `limits.running_fit` hold a `fit` designation and either a part `pair` or feature endpoints `hole` and `shaft` (`PN`, optional `occurrence: "@id"`, `feature`, optional `region`); `limits.heat_set` lists inserts as `{pair: [host, insert], hole_d, insert, source}` with optional `hole_tol`, `depth_min` and `note`. A pair item is a PN placed at most once or an `"@id"`; every PN must name a part record, and a fit on one occurrence never covers another occurrence of its PN. A `from`/`to` starting with `@` must name an occurrence.
  - A clearance requirement is a `type: clearance` interface. Its top-level `pair: [REF, REF]` names two parts, and its `min_clearance_mm` is above 0. A clearance interface without `pair` stays descriptive. Example:

    ```yaml
    - {id: IF-006, from: valve-3, to: piston-3, type: clearance, description: Valve 3 clears the piston crown., pair: ["@valve-3", "@piston-3"], min_clearance_mm: 1.0}
    ```

  - `validate.py` rejects a requirement with an unresolved reference or a value of 0 or less. It also rejects two references to one occurrence, and a second requirement on one occurrence pair.
  - cad-model `check` holds a requirement at the nominal pose, and cad-motion `sweep.py` at every sample. A joint or a fit on the pair does not exempt it.
- `assembly.yaml`: optional top-level `occurrences` (`id` lowercase, `PN`, optional `placement`: `translation_mm` and `rotation: {axis, angle_deg}`, rotation about the part's local origin first), subassemblies listing `parts` (each PN one implicit occurrence at the identity) and/or `occurrences: ["@id", ...]` (each explicit occurrence in exactly one subassembly; a PN with explicit occurrences is never also in `parts`), and joints with travel limits. `scripts/assembly_data.py` is the shared normalizer and reference resolver.
- `parts/<PN>/part.yaml` optional `model_frame`: `assembly_zero` (default; geometry in assembly coordinates at the zero pose, one identity-placed occurrence) or `part_local` (the part's own frame, placed by its occurrences).
- `parts/<PN>/part.yaml`: part number and revision, material, process, finish, mass target in grams, make/buy, optional supplier, and optional `decisions: [DR-###, ...]` references validated against `hw/decisions/`; `model.py` is the parametric CAD source. In the lamp-arm template, F3 reference-mass targets are provisional and documented in `decisions/DR-002.md`; `decisions/DR-004.md` records the catalogue parts, their heat-set seats, and the tolerances proposed for review.
  - Optional `procurement`: `mode` (`catalog` needs `make_buy: buy`; `made_to_drawing`), manufacturer and supplier part numbers, `designation`, `specification` (`reference`, `revision`, an `artifact` file under `hw/` and its `sha256`, which must match), `approved_sources`, `qualification_evidence`, `certificates`.
  - Optional `drawing`: `type` (`detail` for a made-to-drawing part; `vendor_specification` or `source_control` for a catalog item), `tolerance_policy`, `datum_features`, `coordinate_frame`, `views`, `features`, `dimensions` (requests without values, because every value comes from the model), structured `notes`, `manufacturing`, and `sheet`. `validate.py` checks that `datum_features` declares every datum label in use, that view and dimension ids are unique, and that each named tolerance policy exists in product.yaml and applies to the part's process.
- `bom.csv`: hierarchical EBOM; values and fields are checked against part records.
- `verification.csv`: requirement verification status and evidence; every requirement needs a row before release.
- `decisions/DR-###.md`: dated rationale and consequences for design decisions.
- `releases/<rev>/manifest.json`: immutable release file inventory (paths relative to the project root) and check outcomes. `validate.py` requires the directory and manifest revisions to match, inventory files to exist, verification results to be `pass` or `waived`, and no part to remain at an `X` prototype revision.
- `.cad/recheck.json`: staged-index recheck stamp generated by `cad-recheck/scripts/stamp.py`; its JSON records the index tree hash, stage, checks, pass status, timestamp, and tool versions.
- `concept/<scene>.scene.json`: L0 variants and rough primitives; validate it with `cad-concept` against the bundled scene schema.
- `concept/handoff.json`: chosen variant's flattened hierarchy, envelopes, joints, and alternatives; `validate.py` checks it against the adjacent `cad-concept` handoff schema when available, otherwise reports an informational skip.
- `concept/screenshots/`: PNG captures and pose JSON saved by the concept viewer.
- `assets/docs-templates/` contains the GitBuilding build guide, DIN SPEC 3105-1 checklist, and OKH manifest templates.

## Rules

MUST Validate the complete record before release; repair every reported finding before issuing a release manifest.
NOT Treat a nominal joint position outside its stated travel range as valid.
